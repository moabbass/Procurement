### Student email: *maga2@student.le.ac.uk*

### Project description: 
Creating a website for an organisation to make its procurements, the company would make their procurements offer or needs: example (product: laptops of a certain type or details, price range, number of devices required, end date). 

A set of qualified registered suppliers would make their offers by sending documents of their history in the business, the catalogues of the product the organisation needs and the price they demand for it. These offers will be closed the organisation will be unable to view the offers received till the end date of the bid made.


Once its past the due date, the offers will be able to be viewed by the organization, and they will analyse the offers and decide on the supplier they would like to deal with for this transaction.
